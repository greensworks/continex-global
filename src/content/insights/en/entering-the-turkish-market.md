Türkiye is a large, industrialised market with a demanding buyer base. International companies that succeed here tend to share three habits.

## They choose the partner before the product pitch

Distributors, OEM accounts and joint-venture candidates in Türkiye expect a long-term commitment. Companies that map candidates, meet decision-makers in person and agree a cooperation model early move faster than those that lead with a catalogue.

## They treat qualification as a project

Supplier qualification at Turkish OEMs and Tier-1s is a multi-month process with technical, commercial and compliance gates. Someone local has to own the timeline.

## They keep senior people in the conversation

Turkish counterparts want to see that the decision-maker on the other side is engaged. A local interface with direct access to that decision-maker shortens every cycle.
