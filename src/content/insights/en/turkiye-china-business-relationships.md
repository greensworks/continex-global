Chinese technology and EPC companies are among the most active foreign players in Turkish energy and infrastructure. The relationships that work are rarely the result of a single introduction.

## Both sides need a structured interface

Language, decision cycles and contract expectations differ. A coordinating party that understands both sides keeps technical evaluation, commercial negotiation and documentation moving in parallel.

## Start with a defined opportunity

Broad memoranda of understanding rarely convert. A specific project, tender or supply requirement gives both parties a reason to invest time.

## Plan for execution from day one

Financing, local content, logistics and approvals decide whether a signed agreement becomes a delivered project. These belong in the first conversation, not the last.
